Hearts of Venus original JSON excerpts used for the isolated preview.

0025_game009.json and 0014_GUI006.json are captured original JSON resource chunks, complete and valid as JSON individually. They are selected chunks from a partial capture, not a complete provider bundle. hearts-of-venus-preview-animation-extract.json documents clip names and keyframes used/inspected.

The 5x4 preview adapts the source-game stop0–stop4 reel rebound measurements into the shared Phaser motor as a small display-cell proportion, staggered stop timing, and settle delay. This is not literal playback of the original Unity animation or Spine assets. Other source clips are archived for future compatibility assessment; their original serialized curves are not all rendered by the preview.
